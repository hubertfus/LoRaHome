### The blocker, stated once

Item 2 is not merely waiting for hardware. **The Bridge does not report its free
heap over the link**, so even with two boards on the bench the soak would return
`SKIPPED` for heap drift rather than a number — and heap drift is the item the
roadmap singles out:

> `heap_free.end - heap_free.start` is the most important number in the whole
> project. If the drift is non-zero we have a leak and the release must not be
> tagged.

Closing it needs a diagnostic frame carrying `lh_mem_snapshot_t` (§0.4) from the
Bridge to the Host. That work is not in Etap 1's task list, so it is recorded
here and in `docs/test-bench.md` §P5 rather than quietly absorbed.

### One correction that outlives this release

A 230 B frame at the chosen SF9/BW125/CR4-5 profile occupies the air for
**1147.9 ms**, not the ~390 ms carried in the planning notes — that figure is
SF7. Under the ETSI 1% duty cycle this permits roughly **one full frame every
114.8 s** per channel. The calculation was never wrong (C and TypeScript have
always followed AN1200.22 and agree across a 336-point grid); only the number
quoted in prose was. Anything sized against 390 ms — transmit intervals, the
402 ms end-to-end latency target, how many config fragments fit in an hour —
needs re-deriving before Etap 2 builds on it.
